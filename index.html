Is It Time For A Hearing Assessment?

Small changes in hearing can be easy to overlook.

Many people adapt to gradual hearing changes without realizing how much effort they are putting into listening. Consider an assessment if any of these situations sound familiar.

01

Frequently asking people to repeat themselves

Conversations may become harder to follow, especially when several people are speaking.

02

Increasing the volume

You may find yourself turning up the television, phone or other devices more than others prefer.

03

Difficulty in noisy places

Restaurants, family gatherings and busy environments may require extra concentration to understand speech.

04

Missing parts of conversations

You may hear that someone is speaking but struggle to clearly understand what they are saying.

05

Avoiding social situations

Listening difficulties can sometimes make social activities feel tiring or frustrating.

06

Ringing or buzzing sounds

Persistent tinnitus or other unusual sounds may be worth discussing with a hearing professional.

Hearing Affects More Than One Person

Supporting a loved one through hearing loss.

Hearing difficulties can affect family conversations, social activities and everyday communication. Sometimes family members notice changes before the person experiencing them does.

If you are concerned about a parent, spouse, grandparent or another loved one, encourage an open conversation about their hearing.

  • Difficulty following conversations
  • Frequently asking others to repeat themselves
  • Increasing television volume
  • Avoiding group conversations
  • Complaints about ringing or buzzing
