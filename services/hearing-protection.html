Frequently Asked Questions

Hearing protection questions.

Why do I need custom hearing protection?

Custom hearing protection provides a perfect seal and comfort that generic earplugs can't match. They're designed specifically for your ears, offering superior protection and comfort for all-day wear.

How are custom earplugs made?

We take impressions of your ears using a soft, safe material. These impressions are then used to create custom-moulded plugs that fit your ears perfectly for maximum comfort and protection.

How long do custom earplugs last?

With proper care, custom earplugs can last 3-5 years or more. We recommend regular check-ups to ensure they continue to fit well and provide optimal protection.

Can I use custom earplugs for swimming?

Yes, we offer specialised swim plugs that create a watertight seal to prevent water from entering the ear canal, helping prevent swimmer's ear and other water-related ear conditions.

How much does custom hearing protection cost?

The cost varies depending on the type of protection you need and the features you choose. We offer competitive pricing and can discuss your options during a consultation.
