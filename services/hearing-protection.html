Hearing Protection

From workplaces and workshops to concerts and recreation, thoughtful hearing protection can help reduce unnecessary exposure to loud sound.

Know Your Exposure

Where does loud sound show up?

Hearing protection starts with recognizing the environments where your ears are doing too much work.

01

At work

Machinery, tools and industrial environments can create sustained noise exposure that calls for appropriate workplace protection.

02

At events

Concerts, sporting events and entertainment venues can create intense sound levels, especially when you are close to speakers.

03

At play

Power tools, recreational equipment and other hobbies can produce loud or sudden sounds that deserve attention.

Everyday Protection

Protection should fit the activity.

The best approach depends on the environment, the intensity of the sound and how long you are exposed. In some situations, you also need to hear speech, alarms or other important sounds.

WorkshopsReduce exposure around tools and machinery.
Live musicPlan ahead for high-volume events.
RecreationConsider protection around loud equipment.
Daily habitsLower volume and take breaks when possible.

Safe Listening Habits

Small choices can reduce exposure.

Hearing protection is one part of healthy hearing habits. When possible, combine protection with lower volume, shorter exposure and greater distance from loud sources.

Turn it downLower volume when you control the sound.
Take breaksGive your ears time away from loud environments.
Move awayDistance from a loud source can reduce exposure.
Plan aheadBring suitable protection before entering a noisy environment.
Protect consistentlyProtection is most useful when used correctly whenever it is needed.
Ask questionsDiscuss your specific environment if you are unsure what approach is suitable.
