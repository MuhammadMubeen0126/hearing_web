Comprehensive Testing

Testing focused on your hearing needs.

Your hearing professional may recommend different tests depending on your age, hearing concerns and individual requirements.

Pure Tone Hearing Test

Measures how softly you can hear different tones and frequencies. The results help establish your hearing levels and identify areas where hearing support may be beneficial.

Speech Understanding

Helps determine how well you understand spoken words and conversations. This can provide useful information when considering hearing aid technology.

Middle Ear Testing

Where appropriate, additional testing can help assess middle ear function and provide further information about your hearing health.

Could Hearing Aids Help?

Small changes can make a big difference.

If hearing difficulties are affecting your everyday communication, hearing aid testing can help you understand whether hearing technology may be useful.

✓ You regularly ask people to repeat themselves.
✓ Conversations are difficult in noisy environments.
✓ You turn the television or phone volume up.
✓ You struggle to follow group conversations.
✓ You find certain voices difficult to understand.
✓ Family or friends have commented on your hearing.

Not sure which hearing aid is right for you?

You don't need to know which hearing aid you need before making an appointment. Our hearing professionals can assess your hearing, explain your options and help you understand what may work best for your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Hearing aid testing questions.

What is hearing aid testing?

Hearing aid testing involves evaluating your hearing and communication needs to help determine whether hearing aids may be appropriate and which features may best support you.

Do I need a hearing test before choosing hearing aids?

A hearing evaluation is an important part of understanding your hearing needs. Your results can help guide the selection and programming of suitable hearing technology.

Are hearing aids suitable for everyone?

Hearing needs vary from person to person. A professional assessment can help determine whether hearing aids may be appropriate for your individual situation.

Can hearing aids help in noisy environments?

Modern hearing aids can include features designed to support speech understanding and listening comfort in different environments. Your hearing professional can explain which features may be appropriate for you.

Can I bring someone with me?

Absolutely. A family member or friend is welcome to attend if you would find their support helpful, particularly when discussing your communication needs and hearing goals.
