Frequently Asked Questions

Hearing aid fitting questions.

What happens during a hearing aid fitting?

During a fitting, your hearing professional will place and adjust your hearing aids for comfort, program them based on your hearing test results, and test them in real-world listening scenarios to ensure optimal performance.

How long does a hearing aid fitting take?

A comprehensive hearing aid fitting typically takes 60-90 minutes. This allows time for physical fitting, programming, real-world testing, and answering any questions you may have.

How often should I have my hearing aids re-fitted?

We recommend annual check-ups to ensure your hearing aids continue to perform optimally. You may also need a re-fitting if your hearing changes, you get new devices, or if you experience comfort issues.

Will I need follow-up appointments?

Yes, we typically schedule a follow-up appointment 2-4 weeks after your initial fitting. This allows time to adjust to your hearing aids and make any necessary refinements.

Can my existing hearing aids be re-fitted?

Absolutely. We can re-fit and reprogram your existing hearing aids to improve comfort and performance, especially if your hearing levels have changed or you're experiencing new listening challenges.
