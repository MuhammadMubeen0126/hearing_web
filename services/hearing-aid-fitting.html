Personalized Hearing Care

Hearing Aid Fitting

A hearing aid should feel like part of your routine—not something you have to think about all day. Our fitting process focuses on comfort, clarity and confidence.

Talk About Your Hearing Needs
Why Fitting Matters

The device is only the beginning.

Good hearing technology needs to be fitted, programmed and refined around the person wearing it.

01

Personal Programming

Settings are based on your hearing profile and refined through your experience with everyday sound.

02

Physical Comfort

We pay attention to fit, handling and the practical details that make wearing your devices easier.

03

Confidence

You receive guidance on controls, charging or batteries, cleaning and everyday use.

Your Fitting Journey

From first fit to fine-tuning.

Our approach gives you time to understand your hearing aids and become comfortable using them.

1

Review

We revisit your hearing results, listening priorities and the environments where you most want to hear comfortably.

2

Fit & Program

Your devices are fitted and programmed so you can begin experiencing amplification appropriate to your hearing needs.

3

Learn

We show you how to insert, remove, charge or change batteries, care for your devices and use important controls.

4

Fine-Tune

As you use your hearing aids, your feedback helps identify settings that may need refinement.

Designed Around Real Life

Tell us where hearing matters most.

Hearing technology should be considered in the context of your actual life. Your priorities may be very different from someone else's.

Family Conversations Following several people at once.
Restaurants Managing speech with background noise.
Television Finding comfortable listening levels.
Telephone Making calls more manageable.
Ongoing Support

Your first settings aren't necessarily your final settings.

As you spend time with your hearing aids, you may discover specific situations where you want more clarity, less background sound or a different level of comfort.

  • Review how your hearing aids feel
  • Discuss sound quality and listening situations
  • Make appropriate programming adjustments
  • Check cleaning, fit and device care
  • Answer questions about everyday use
