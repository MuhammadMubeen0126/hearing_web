FAQs

Common repair questions.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are completed within 24-48 hours. Complex repairs may take longer, and we'll keep you informed throughout the process.

Do you repair all hearing aid brands?

Yes, we repair all major hearing aid brands including Phonak, Oticon, Signia, Starkey, Widex, and many others.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes, all our repairs come with a 90-day warranty on parts and labour. We stand behind the quality of our work.

What if my hearing aids are still under warranty?

We'll check your warranty status and advise you on the best course of action. We can often work with manufacturers to honour warranty coverage.
