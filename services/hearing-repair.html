Something Not Working?

Hearing Aid Repair

Before assuming your hearing aid needs a major repair, let's identify what changed. Some problems are related to cleaning, power, accessories or settings.

Get Repair Help
Common Symptoms

What problem are you experiencing?

Knowing the symptom helps us start troubleshooting in the right place.

01 · Sound

Weak or quiet

Sound suddenly seems lower than usual or one device is not producing the expected volume.

02 · Feedback

Whistling

Unexpected feedback can have several causes, including fit, buildup or device-related issues.

03 · Power

Won't turn on

Battery and charging connections are among the first things to investigate when a device appears inactive.

04 · Connection

Wireless issues

Connectivity problems can involve the hearing aid, accessory, phone or settings rather than a physical failure.

05 · Maintenance

Blocked openings

Debris or buildup can affect sound delivery and should be addressed using the correct care procedure.

06 · Physical

Damage or wear

If a component is damaged or worn, a professional assessment can help determine the appropriate service.

Before Your Visit

A few simple checks can save time.

If your device has stopped working as expected, note what changed and whether the issue affects one or both hearing aids.

Follow the care instructions for your specific model. Avoid opening or modifying the hearing aid yourself.

Things to note

  • When did the problem start?
  • Is one or both devices affected?
  • Does the problem come and go?
  • Has the device been exposed to moisture?
  • Is the battery or charger behaving differently?
  • Did the problem start after a drop or impact?

Long-Term Care

Repair, maintain or replace?

A problem does not automatically mean it is time for a new device.

Maintenance may be enough

Cleaning, replacing routine components, checking power and addressing simple settings can sometimes restore expected performance.

Professional service may be needed

If the device has physical damage, persistent performance problems or an internal fault, further service may be appropriate.
